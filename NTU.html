Overview

Studying at NTU marked my first time living abroad alone for an extended period, immersed in a culture and language different from my own. Beyond any single project, this experience taught me how to navigate unfamiliar environments with confidence — engaging with people from diverse backgrounds, adapting to new ways of thinking, and broadening my perspective on how problems can be approached differently across cultures. It was a small but meaningful step toward the wider world, one that shaped how I collaborate with people from different backgrounds in every project since.

Life in a New Environment

Living and studying in Singapore exposed me to a genuinely multicultural environment, with classmates and collaborators from across Southeast Asia and beyond. Communicating and working across language and cultural differences pushed me to become a more adaptable communicator, and gave me a firsthand sense of how the same technical problem can be approached from very different starting assumptions depending on one's background.
